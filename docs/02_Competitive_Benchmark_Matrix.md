# Competitive Benchmark Matrix

15 references. 8 ecommerce, 7 non-ecommerce. Full per-reference analysis in `01_Phase1_Discovery_Report.md` §2.
This matrix is the scannable summary and the adoption decision record.

**None of these brands' visual language, copy, or layout is to be copied.** Only abstracted patterns are adopted, and every adoption is restated as an original Tabasamu principle (P-01 … P-15).

## Matrix D — Explicit anti-patterns (do not build)

| # | Anti-pattern | Observed in | Why it is banned here |
|---|---|---|---|
| AP-01 | Countdown timers, "only 2 left!", scarcity badges | E-01, E-08 | Brand voice: *"the voice of someone already at ease."* |
| AP-02 | Exit-intent modals, social-proof popups | E-04 | Same. Also hostile on mobile. |
| AP-03 | Checkout upsells / order bumps | E-04 | Premium positioning collapses at the moment of trust. |
| AP-04 | Quiz-led product discovery | E-04 | Instructive, not invitational. Brand voice bans "you should". |
| AP-05 | Splash / pour / frozen-droplet photography | E-01 | Brand Book §05 explicit ban. |
| AP-06 | Payment vendor's own UI at checkout | N-04 | P-08. |
| AP-07 | Parallax, scroll-jacking, entrance animations | N-04, N-07 | P-11. |
| AP-08 | Colour as the sole carrier of meaning | E-01 | WCAG 1.4.1; and gold fails contrast entirely. |
| AP-09 | Box builder that breaks below 400px or on keyboard | E-07 | P-09; primary device is a mid-range Android. |
| AP-10 | Delivery cost revealed only at checkout | *KE market norm* | P-03. The single biggest first-time-buyer frustration. |
| AP-11 | "Artisan empowerment" / hands-of-the-farmer framing | N-06 | Brand Book: *"without translation, without apology."* |
| AP-12 | Pure white cards, modals, or input fields | *ubiquitous* | Brand Book §03: pure white is forbidden. |
