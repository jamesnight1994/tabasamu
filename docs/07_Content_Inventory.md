## 5. The two content contradictions that block writing

> These are not editorial preferences. They are contradictions **inside the binding document**, and no page can be written honestly until they are resolved.

**⚠ D-50 — Rooibos vs hibiscus.**
The Brand Book's origin story says the brand was built fermenting *"Kenyan-grown hibiscus, ginger, and turmeric"*, and its own voice example cites *"Hibiscus from Kerio Valley"*. But the product — every label, the packaging spec, the descriptor — is **rooibos** kombucha. **Rooibos is South African, not Kenyan-grown.** The mission claims *"rooted in Kenyan soil"*; the base ingredient is not. This is the brand's central claim, and it is currently unsupportable as written. It must be resolved by the client before Our Story or Ingredients can be written.

**⚠ D-52 — Six days or fourteen?**
The Brand Book voice example: *"Six days in the jar."* The Strategy document: *"naturally fermented for 14 days."* Specificity is the brand's stated trust mechanism (*"Specificity earns trust. Names the place."*). A specific number that is wrong is worse than no number. **The client must state the actual fermentation period.**
