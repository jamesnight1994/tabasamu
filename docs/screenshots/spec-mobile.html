The short version

Three things worth knowing.

01

No caffeine

Rooibos has none to begin with, so none has to be taken out. You can drink this at four in the afternoon and still sleep.

02

Small batches

We brew in quantities small enough that every batch is tasted before it leaves. When a flavour sells out, it is because it sold out.

03

Kenyan fruit

Passion, pineapple, beetroot, gooseberry, grape. Grown here, pressed here, and added after the ferment so the flavour stays bright.

What goes in

Fruit first, then patience.

Every bottle starts the same way: rooibos, steeped and cooled, then fermented with a culture we have kept alive since the first kitchen batch. The fruit goes in last, once the ferment has done its work, which is why the flavour still tastes like the fruit and not like the fermentation.

Where it began

A kitchen in Nairobi.

It started because someone wanted a drink with a bit of complexity to it, and did not want to be awake at midnight for the privilege.

Rooibos was the answer to the caffeine. The fruit was the answer to everything else — and the fruit was already here, at the market, better than anything that could be shipped in.

The batches got bigger. The kitchen did not.

Rooted in the soil, crafted for the soul.
